🍝 Classic Spaghetti Carbonara

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 150g pancetta or guanciale, diced
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • ¾ cup grated Parmesan or Pecorino Romano cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook spaghetti until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water, then drain.
  2. While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add pancetta (and garlic if using) and cook until crisp. Remove garlic afterward.
  3. In a bowl, whisk together eggs, yolk, and grated cheese until smooth.
  4. Add the hot drained pasta to the pan with pancetta. Toss to coat in the fat and remove from heat.
  5. Quickly pour the egg and cheese mixture into the pasta, stirring constantly to create a creamy sauce. Add a splash of reserved pasta water as needed to loosen the sauce.
  6. Season generously with black pepper and adjust salt to taste.
  7. Serve immediately with extra cheese and pepper on top. 🇮🇹

Pro Tips

  • Use freshly grated cheese for the best flavor and texture.
  • Don’t scramble the eggs — remove the pan from heat before adding the egg mixture.
  • Reserve enough pasta water; it’s the secret to a silky, creamy sauce.
  • Traditional carbonara doesn’t use cream — the creaminess comes from eggs and cheese!
